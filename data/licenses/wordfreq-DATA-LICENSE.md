# Wordfreq-derived data attribution

The supplemental vocabulary comes from Allison Parrish's
[wordfreq-en-25000 export](https://github.com/aparrish/wordfreq-en-25000),
which credits Robyn Speer's [wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq).

The export's data is made available under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The [license text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode)
and [wordfreq's source attributions](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq#sources-and-supported-languages)
describe its terms and underlying sources.

KeySprint modifications: retain lowercase ASCII words, exclude whole-word
blocklist matches, remove duplicates, and append eligible words after the
Google no-swears base. Then remove every practice word shorter than three
letters from all pools without replacement. Original frequency numbers are omitted.
The supplemental contribution remains subject to CC BY-SA 4.0. The Google
contribution has the separate notice preserved beside this file.
